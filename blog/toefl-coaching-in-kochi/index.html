TOEFL Coaching in Kochi: 2026 Format and Preparation Guide

By Sreya 22 November 2024 6 min read

TOEFL iBT is one of the most widely accepted English tests for university admission, especially in the United States. If you are a student in Kochi or anywhere in Kerala planning to study abroad, you need to know that the test changed significantly in January 2026. This guide explains who needs TOEFL, what the current test looks like, what good TOEFL coaching in Kochi should cover, and how long you should allow for preparation.

Who needs TOEFL iBT?

TOEFL is designed to measure the English you will use at university: reading academic texts, following lectures, writing clearly and speaking with confidence. It is most commonly required by universities in the United States, and it is also accepted by many universities in Canada, the UK, Australia, Europe and elsewhere.

You are likely to need TOEFL if:

  • You are applying for undergraduate or postgraduate study in the US.
  • Your chosen university lists TOEFL iBT as one of its accepted English tests and you prefer its format.
  • You are applying for a scholarship or programme that specifically asks for TOEFL.

Before you book, check two things. First, confirm the minimum score your university asks for, and whether it now quotes requirements on the new 1 to 6 scale. Second, if you also need a visa or immigration test, check that separately, because visa rules can differ from university rules. For example, Canada’s Express Entry programme for permanent residence accepts CELPIP-General, IELTS General Training and PTE Core for English, not TOEFL.

The current TOEFL iBT format (from January 2026)

ETS updated TOEFL iBT in January 2026 with new task types, adaptive Reading and Listening sections and a new score scale. Much of the material online still describes the older test, so make sure your preparation matches the version below, taken from ETS’s official test content page.

Section Task types Approx. base time
Reading Complete the Words, Read in Daily Life, Read an Academic Passage 30 minutes
Listening Listen and Choose a Response, Listen to a Conversation, Listen to an Announcement, Listen to an Academic Talk 29 minutes
Writing Build a Sentence, Write an Email, Write for an Academic Discussion 23 minutes
Speaking Listen and Repeat, Take an Interview 8 minutes

ETS advises allowing about two hours in total, as times do not include directions. Because Reading and Listening are adaptive, the questions you see later in each section depend on how you performed earlier, so the exact number of items and time can vary.

How TOEFL is scored now

  • You receive a score from 1 to 6 for each of the four sections, in half-band steps.
  • Your overall score is the average of the four sections, rounded to the nearest half band.
  • For a two-year transition period after January 2026, your score report also shows a comparable overall score on the old 0 to 120 scale.

Where you can take it

TOEFL iBT can be taken on a computer at an authorised test centre, or as the TOEFL iBT Home Edition on your own computer with a live human proctor. If you choose the Home Edition, check the equipment and room requirements well before your test date.

What good TOEFL coaching in Kochi should cover

Reading: speed and everyday texts

The new Reading section mixes academic passages with everyday texts such as notices, emails and messages, plus a Complete the Words task that tests spelling, grammar and vocabulary in context. Coaching should build reading speed, vocabulary and the habit of using context clues.

Listening: academic and everyday English

You will hear short exchanges, conversations, campus-style announcements and academic talks. Good coaching trains you to catch the speaker’s purpose, attitude and key details, and to take short, useful notes.

Writing: accuracy and clear organisation

Build a Sentence tests grammar and word order. Write an Email tests whether you can write a clear, polite message for a real purpose. Write for an Academic Discussion asks you to add your opinion to an online class discussion. Our post on common TOEFL writing mistakes made by Indian students covers errors worth avoiding.

Here is an example of a short, well-organised email for a prompt asking you to write to a professor about missing a class:

Dear Professor,

I am writing to let you know that I was unable to attend Tuesday’s lecture because I had a high fever. I have already borrowed notes from a classmate, but I am unsure about the second part of the assignment you explained in class. Could you tell me whether there is a recording of the lecture, or suggest a time when I could ask you a few questions? Thank you for your understanding.

Kind regards, [Your name]

It states the purpose in the first line, covers every point in the prompt, and keeps a polite, natural tone.

Speaking: natural, confident responses

Listen and Repeat tests how accurately you can reproduce what you hear, which depends on listening, pronunciation and rhythm. Take an Interview asks you to answer questions on everyday and academic topics. Regular speaking practice with feedback on pronunciation, fluency and organisation is essential.

Adaptive test strategy and time management

In an adaptive test you cannot rely on easy questions later to make up for careless mistakes early. Coaching should help you stay accurate from the very first question and manage your pace across the whole test.

Mock tests and feedback

Practise with official ETS material for the updated test, and review every mock test with a trainer so you know which skills to target next.

How long should you prepare?

Your timeline depends on your current level and your target score. As a general guide:

  • Strong English, moderate target: a few weeks of focused practice to learn the new tasks and timing.
  • Good English, high target for competitive universities: allow more time for consistent practice in writing and speaking.
  • Weaker grammar or vocabulary: start with general English skills first. Our Communicative English course can help you build fluency before test practice.

Work backwards from your application deadlines, and leave time for a possible retake before scores are due.

Frequently asked questions

Is the old TOEFL score of 0 to 120 still used?

ETS now reports scores on a 1 to 6 scale. For a two-year transition period after January 2026, score reports also include a comparable 0 to 120 overall score, so universities that still quote the old scale can compare. Check how your university states its requirement.

Should I take TOEFL or IELTS?

Both are widely accepted for university admission. Choose based on what your universities and any visa route accept, and on which format suits you. If you are applying mainly to US universities, TOEFL is a natural choice. If you need a test for immigration, check the official list for that country first.

Can I prepare for TOEFL online?

Yes. TOEFL is a computer-based test, and live online sessions let you practise speaking, get writing feedback and take timed practice tests without travelling. One-on-one sessions also let your trainer adapt each class to your progress.
