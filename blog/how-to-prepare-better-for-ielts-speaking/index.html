What do most people think about IELTS Speaking Test?

IELTS speaking is the shortest of all the four modules of IELTS test. Regarding this, many aspirants consider this as a bone of contention. Due to this they will consider in getting average scores rather than a good performance. When you consider modules other than IELTS speaking, you are well aware that you are not being judged directly. But, when you consider the IELTS speaking section, you will be face to face with the examiner. This will be difficult for a person who is not fluent in English or for those who have trouble for good English conversation.

But the happy part is the IELTS Speaking test will be held for only 11 – 14 minutes. Here you have to maintain that natural speech throughout. The speaking test is divided into three sections and can be scored on a nine-band scale. The three sections are as follows:

  • Introduction and Interview Questions
  • CUE Card Task
  • Counter Questions/ Discussions

Now let us have a detailed discussion regarding these three sections of IELTS speaking and know how to prepare correctly.

Introduction and Interview Questions

The examiner will first introduce them and will ask the candidate questions on some familiar topics. Here we can see few topics in which you must get familiarized for the IELTS speaking exam. Some common speaking questions which are asked include: What do you do? Where are you coming from? What are your hobbies? Why select a peculiar field of study? How do you utilize your free time? Which town are you from? Is it a good place to live and why?

There are some questions for which you can prepare the answers before so that you can crystal clear answer. No matter what the question is, never answer it in one or two sentences. Instead of this you can elaborate your view points in 2 or 3 sentences. They must be crisp and complete. You must learn to speak in a very relaxed and composed way.

CUE Card Task

In case of IELTS speaking you will get approximately one minute to prepare. The instructions to guide your talk are written on a card. You should speak for two minutes until the examiner asks you to stop.

Counter Questions or Discussion

After the CUE Card tsk, the examiner will ask you counter questions. It will be based in the second task. Here questions will be co-related to the CUE Card topic and for each questions you must answer in few sentences.
